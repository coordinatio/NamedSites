NamedSites

Adds a short prefix to the browser window title when the active tab matches one of your rules — so environments like RC: are instantly recognizable in the taskbar and window switcher.

    Hostname, e.g. rc.contentai.ru — matches the domain and all its subdomains.

    “RC:” is applied as “RC: ” (a trailing space is added automatically). New rules are enabled and added at the bottom of the list.

    Where you’ll see it: prefixes are visible wherever your OS shows window titles — the taskbar, alt-tab / window switcher, wmctrl -l. Some desktop environments don’t draw a title bar in the window frame itself; that’s expected, not a bug.

    Private windows: they require the “Run in Private Windows” grant — open about:addons → NamedSites → Allow. Without it, private tabs are invisible to the extension.
